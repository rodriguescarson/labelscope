Blocked split

The split nnU-Net writes when none is supplied — KFold(5, shuffle=True, random_state=12345) over the sorted case names — puts 91.9% of validation patches in contact with voxels the model trained on, sharing 23.1% of their volume on average and up to 57.3% at worst. Averaged over 200 random shuffles the figure is 91.9%, so this is a property of the data, not of one unlucky seed. The blocked split written alongside this report removes that contact entirely.
