Offset is measured along the surface normal, in voxels, positive outward, by averaging intensity profiles over a cube of labelled surface and locating the peak of the average. A per-voxel nearest-maximum would be meaningless here — on carbonised papyrus its value tracks the search radius rather than any displacement, because fibre maxima, both faces of the sheet and the neighbouring wrap all sit within the window. The naive_* columns in the CSV keep that measure alongside, for comparison only.
